FOR THE LOVE OF A PIT

She was never meant to live. She lived anyway.

This is Duchess. She is why I photograph pit bulls the way I do — and why the dog is never extra in a family picture.

Dedication

Mark found Duchess in the Bronx. She was the runt of a fighting litter. They were going to use her as bait. She was not meant to survive.

He brought her home and gave her the best life he could. Then we both did. She was goofy and gentle and she stayed. Duchess is why I fell in love with pit bulls.

This page is for her. For Snickers, a boy who came from a puppy mill and still found a way to be himself. For Pumpkin, who is with me now. And for every dog still waiting.

The Work

Pit bulls as they really are.

I photograph them gentle, goofy, loyal, and loved. A scared dog shot in a kennel gets passed over. A good photograph gets a second look.

I get down on the ground, at their eye level, and I wait. I shoot on my phone. This isn't about money — except when you hire me for a family, and even then the dog comes too.

Hire Me

Mini family sessions. The animal is a must.

I photograph families the way I photograph wildlife — quietly, at their level, with whoever they love in the frame. Children, parents, grandparents. Black and white.

The dog is not optional. Cats, horses, the one who sleeps on the bed — if they are family, they are in the picture. If you want portraits without them, I am not the photographer.
